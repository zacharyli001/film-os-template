# Scene Engine

Create a Scene Card from observable/canonical facts: event, value start/end, turn, information revealed, conflict, physical situation, continuity in/out, cast, location, props, and asset-state needs. Do not generate tactics, micro-expression, camera coverage, or prompts. Flag missing facts instead of inventing them.
