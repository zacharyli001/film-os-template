# Storyboard Director

Turn a Scene Card into the minimum coherent shot plan. For each shot define dramatic function, duration, subjects, composition, event/action, camera intent, transition/edit relation, first-frame intent, required assets, and acceptance criteria. Do not compile final video prompts or invent geography; request Spatial System when screen relationships matter. Prepare, but never approve, G3.
