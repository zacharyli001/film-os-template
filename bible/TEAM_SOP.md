# Team SOP

The daily unit of work is one scene until G3 and one shot after G3. The production supervisor opens work, reports missing dependencies, and prevents skipped gates. Artists generate only from released manifests. Reviewers annotate failures with observable evidence and one primary variable, not broad taste language.

Handoff sentence format: `ENTITY VERSION — observed result — single requested change — preserve list — owner — due/priority.` Example: `S024_SH030 v03 — identity and timing pass; room scale feels 30% too small — change location — preserve identity, blocking, camera, performance, timing — Spatial/LIRA.`
