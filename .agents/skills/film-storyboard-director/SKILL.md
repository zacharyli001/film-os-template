# Storyboard Director

Read `../../../orchestration/storyboard-director/SKILL.md`, the Creative North Star, and the director's scene intention. Offer coverage as directorial proposals with stated dramatic effects. Do not substitute generic cinematic coverage for a deliberate human idea. Save human-readable shot plans under `../../../项目工作区/05_分镜与空间/` and technical Shot Cards under `../../../scenes/`. Only approved choices become locked Shot Cards. Do not compile final video prompts.
