# Writers' Room

Use when two or more people are discussing a premise, character problem, season, episode, scene sequence, alternative, or revision. Read `../../../AGENTS.md`, `../../../CREATIVE_CONSTITUTION.zh-CN.md`, the project overview, Creative North Star, decision record, and the minimum approved story records relevant to the room's declared scope.

The room supports human authorship; it does not decide by vote, model confidence, volume of notes, or apparent consensus. Begin with one room question, the decision authority, what is already locked, what remains open, and what must be delivered. Keep personal pitches, AI expansions, craft observations, and director decisions visibly separate.

Choose the lightest useful room mode:

- `pitch`: collect distinct possibilities without combining them prematurely;
- `break`: turn an approved direction into causal beats, scenes, or episode movements;
- `stress_test`: test logic, originality, character behavior, rhythm, continuity, and production consequences;
- `notes`: classify feedback by symptom, evidence, likely cause, authority, and priority;
- `consolidate`: compare proposals and prepare one short director decision sheet.

Do not produce dozens of cosmetic variants. Preserve minority proposals until the decision authority accepts or rejects them. If a proposed change touches premise, theme, protagonist, ending, core arc, world law, or show-level visual language, label it as a director-level creative decision. Craft leads may resolve only delegated execution choices inside approved intent.

For series work, preserve the hierarchy `series → season → episode → scene`; distinguish episode closure from season continuation; and consult `$film-series-continuity` for knowledge, state, setup/payoff, or retcon impact. Use `$film-series-architect` for permanent engine or season design, `$film-story-architect` for approved story construction, `$film-screenplay-writer` for pages, and `$film-script-doctor` for diagnosis. This skill facilitates collaboration and handoff; it does not duplicate those departments.

Save a human-readable room record under `../../../项目工作区/02_故事架构/编剧室/` only when the repository authority rules allow the current user to write there. Otherwise keep it in the current contributor proposal bundle. Record: scope, participants, canonical base, human inputs, AI expansions, proposals, evidence/tradeoffs, unresolved questions, assignments, decision needed, and any explicit human decision. Discussion and consensus are not approval.

End with a short decision sheet containing: what the room explored, the strongest distinct options, consequences, conflicts with locked material, downstream impact, and exactly one decision requested from the named authority.
