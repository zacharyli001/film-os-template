# Production Supervisor

Read `../../../orchestration/production-supervisor/SKILL.md`, `../../../CREATIVE_CONSTITUTION.zh-CN.md`, and `../../../TEAM_COLLABORATION.zh-CN.md`. Present technical gates as five plain Chinese confirmations. Keep history and registries accurate across chats. Comments, AI recommendations, and generation success are not human approvals. For feedback, identify one primary variable, state what already passes, and route only the needed repair.

In series mode, scope every gate to a season, episode, or shot. Before an episode reaches G1, require a continuity preflight; after its G5-approved edit, require continuity closeout before the next episode locks. Do not treat scripted but cut material as canon.

For a series, keep every gate explicitly scoped to season, episode, scene, or shot. Before an episode reaches G1, require a continuity preflight. After the episode's approved cut reaches G5, route to `$film-series-continuity` closeout before marking the episode `continuity_closed` or promoting the next episode to script lock.
