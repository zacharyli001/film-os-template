# Idea Development Lenses

Use this reference only when a raw idea needs development. Do not run every lens.

## Preserve the human kernel

First capture, in the creator's own terms:

- the image, feeling, question, contradiction, character, relationship, or situation that started the idea;
- what the creator finds exciting, disturbing, funny, beautiful, or personally necessary;
- what must not be normalized, explained away, or replaced;
- what remains genuinely undecided.

For every suggested addition, state its lineage back to that kernel. An attractive idea without lineage is an unrelated pitch.

## Entry lenses

Choose one when the creator knows the attraction but not yet the story:

- **theme tension**: the unresolved human question, without deciding the answer;
- **character pressure**: the person whose way of living becomes impossible to maintain;
- **relationship engine**: two people who need each other for incompatible reasons;
- **place pressure**: an environment whose rules continually force behavior;
- **concept collision**: two normally separate realities, values, scales, or systems forced together.

## Transformation lenses

Use in `expand` or `challenge` mode, never as automatic canon:

- move a familiar behavior into an unfamiliar time, place, body, or scale;
- make a metaphor physically true and test its consequences;
- reverse one social, physical, or narrative rule while preserving the rest;
- combine two approved elements and look for the new conflict created between them;
- keep the premise but change who is allowed to perceive, remember, narrate, or act.

Offer at most three materially different proposals. Avoid cosmetic variants.

## Expression lenses for AI-native film

When form may be part of the idea, test whether the concept is best carried by:

- restricted point of view or interface/screen mediation;
- altered time, repetition, loop, omission, or reverse disclosure;
- scale shifts between macro, human, architectural, and planetary views;
- image/sound disagreement, purposeful silence, or off-screen causality;
- one governing visual rule that changes meaning over the work.

Recommend one primary device and at most one supporting device. Form must intensify the human question, not merely demonstrate a generation technique. Detailed shot design belongs to Storyboard, sound execution to Sound, and video prompt compilation to CINEDANCE.

## Format fit

Assess rather than prescribe:

- **conceptual short**: one strong transformation, image-rule, or experiential turn;
- **narrative short**: one concentrated dramatic change with little structural overhead;
- **standalone film**: a conflict and character transformation that earns sustained escalation and closure;
- **series**: a repeatable source of episode conflict plus longer-running change that does not exhaust after the premise episode.

Duration is a creative constraint. Explain what the idea gains and loses at each plausible scale.
