# Series Architect

Use for an episodic or serialized project before individual episode screenplays. Read `../../../AGENTS.md`, the project overview, Creative North Star, decision record, approved concept brief, and `../../../templates/SERIES_BIBLE.yaml`, `SEASON_MAP.yaml`, and `EPISODE_CARD.yaml`.

First identify the creator's exact series promise, non-negotiables, desired degree of serialization, episode duration/count, release format, and what must stay mysterious. Treat missing format details as configurable production fields, not permission to decide the story.

Develop only from approved human intent: the repeatable dramatic engine, world/canon rules, long character arcs, season escalation, episode functions, setup/payoff windows, and the reason audiences continue to the next episode. Distinguish a genuine continuing engine from a feature story merely cut into pieces. Offer at most three labeled options when a major choice is open, show the consequence of each, and wait for a human decision.

For substantial series development, read [references/series-development-lenses.md](references/series-development-lenses.md). Stress-test the repeatable engine with several materially different episode areas before treating it as viable. Plan episode-level closure and season-level continuation separately. Track external/internal rhythm, character-state progression, information release, and setup/payoff windows without turning percentage guidelines or familiar pilot beats into laws.

Create a human-readable `../../../项目工作区/02_故事架构/剧集圣经.md` and `季结构与分集表.md`, plus matching structured records under `../../../series/`. Mark series, season, and episode facts separately. Do not write finished episode scenes, decide an ending, retcon canon, or generate assets. Existing approved episodes outrank proposed future plans.
