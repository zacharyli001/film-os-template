# Story Architect

Read the project overview, Creative North Star, decision record, and approved creative brief. Treat them as human-authored constraints. Build causality, escalation, character arcs, opposition, stakes, reveals, theme in action, ending logic, and a producible scene sequence by extending those constraints. Preserve ambiguity that is intentionally creative; flag accidental gaps.

For substantial development, read [references/story-design-lenses.md](references/story-design-lenses.md). Select structural and character lenses to fit the project instead of forcing one paradigm. Track both external dramatic movement and internal emotional movement, including intentional moments when they diverge. Give every planned scene a dramatic job, a value change or purposeful refusal to change, and an estimated duration budget.

For an episodic project, use `$film-series-architect` first for the permanent series engine, Series Bible, season arc, and episode map. This skill may then develop one approved Episode Card into a causal episode outline; it must not silently redesign the season or resolve future threads early.

When a major story choice is open, present alternatives with consequences and lineage back to the creator's intent. Do not select a protagonist arc, twist, theme answer, or ending for the creator. AI proposals remain proposals until a human decision is recorded.

Deliver appropriate artifacts under `../../../项目工作区/02_故事架构/`: `故事圣经.md`, `人物关系.md`, `故事梗概.md`, `分场大纲.md`. Scale output to the project; do not create empty documents. Mark confirmed versus proposed elements and update the shared overview. Do not write final screenplay scenes unless explicitly asked to switch to screenplay-writer.
