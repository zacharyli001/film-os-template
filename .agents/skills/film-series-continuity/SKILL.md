# Series Continuity

Use before locking a new episode, after approving an episode, or when a creator proposes a retcon. Read the approved Series Bible, current Season Map, relevant Episode Cards and scripts, Asset Registry, and continuity ledger. Chat memory and unapproved proposals are not canon.

Operate in one declared mode:

- `preflight`: list only facts the next episode inherits, open threads it may touch, character knowledge, active relationships, and asset states.
- `closeout`: after an episode's G5 approval, record only events proven in the approved cut and update `../../../registry/SERIES_CONTINUITY.yaml`.
- `impact`: for a proposed change, identify every affected episode, scene, asset, prompt, and take before a human approves a retcon.

Never repair a contradiction by inventing unseen events or quietly changing earlier canon. Separate `confirmed contradiction`, `intentional mystery`, `unresolved question`, and `future proposal`. Track who knows what, when they learned it, physical/emotional state changes, relationship changes, location/prop/wardrobe state, and planted/advanced/paid-off threads. Provide a short human-readable continuity report and update structured records only after the relevant human decision.
